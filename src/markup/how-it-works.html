    FAQ

    Before you search.

    How often is information updated?

    Official sources are swept twice a day. Each entry shows the date it was last verified, and anything unconfirmed for 30 days is marked "status unverified".

    What happens when a restaurant disputes a report?

    The restaurant can respond publicly, upload evidence, or flag the report as incorrect. Our team re-checks flagged reports and can mark them resolved or not substantiated.

    Can restaurants remove complaints?

    No. Restaurants can respond and provide evidence, but only moderation can change a report's status. Nothing is deleted to make a profile look better.

    What does Platform Verified mean?

    Our team reviewed the restaurant's submitted evidence against the checkup. It is PlateWatch's own review, not a government certificate.

    Is a PlateWatch score an official government rating?

    No. The score is our own, calculated from the restaurant's evidence. Official FSSAI hygiene ratings are shown separately, only when one exists.

    What happens to old regulatory actions?

    They stay in the history with their dates, alongside any reinstatement. The current status always shows the latest official action.
